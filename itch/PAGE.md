## Title (the honest version)

Still Water

## Short description (tagline)

A short fishing tale. A golden fish grants three wishes. It gives you exactly what you ask for.

## Description

A calm day on a still lake. The sun hangs low and white, the water is a mirror, and the fish are biting.

Then you catch a golden fish. Put it back, it says, and it will grant you a wish. Three, if you're patient.

Mind what you think out loud out here. Every wish costs a little daylight, and the fish always keeps its word. It has promised you another sun.

A short game, a few minutes a run, with seven endings. Play it in a desktop browser or on a phone held upright. It has sound; headphones recommended.

## Controls

Tap or click to cast and to strike, hold to reel, let go when it pulls (Space or Enter work too).

## Content note

Unsettling imagery and a slow dread: eyes in the water, teeth, the deep, drowning. No gore, but a few sudden loud moments.
